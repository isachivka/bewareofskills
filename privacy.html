Privacy policy

Beware of Skills · last updated 27 October 2026

Beware of Skills publishes short videos about coding with AI agents on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. To do that, we run a small private publishing tool ("reels-publisher") that uploads our own videos to our own channels.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the tool uses the platforms' official APIs to upload videos and to read the status of the videos it uploaded (for YouTube: the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes). It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other users.

Storage and sharing

The access tokens the platforms issue are stored only on the channel owner's own computers and are never shared with or sold to third parties. Nothing is used for advertising.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in their Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in the settings of the other platforms.

Contact

Questions: through the Beware of Skills YouTube channel.
